A1 only
B1 and 3 only
C✓ 2 and 3 only

📖 Explanation

1 [2020] Indigo cultivation in India declined by the beginning of the 20th century becaus...
›
Indigo cultivation in India declined by the beginning of the 20th century because of:
APeasant resistance to the oppressive conduct of planters
B✓ Its unprofitability in the world market because of new inventions
CNational leaders' opposition to the cultivation of indigo
DGovernment control over the planters
📖 Explanation
Germans succeeded in making synthetic indigo in the laboratory. The breakthrough came in 1890 by Karl Heumann, and large-scale synthetic indigo production began in 1897. The synthetic dye was much cheaper, making natural indigo unprofitable.
